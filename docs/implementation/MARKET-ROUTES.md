# Market routes

Steward's parent-owned account and caregiver controls are intended to work independently of a particular country, issuer, broker or trading venue. **No default launch country has been selected.** An asset route can become available only after its own technical, market and user-eligibility review; one unavailable route does not block the account and record features.

| Route | Current Steward status | What must be established before a real-money offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fake-funds demo and mock-asset testnet | Available for software demonstrations and rehearsals. | Keep the simulated nature clear. These do not qualify a real asset or user. |
| Existing onchain assets | Account and guarded transaction mechanics have been rehearsed locally. No general real-money release is approved. | Verify the particular asset, chain, venue, price sources, contract behavior, market availability and user requirements. |
| Existing stock-linked tokens | A local fork rehearsal exercised buy and sell mechanics, but no public Steward stock-token route is approved. | Review the specific token's rights, issuer and venue terms, permitted countries and users, wallet transfer rules, market state and price protections. Describe token exposure accurately; do not call it ownership of underlying shares unless that is actually the product. |
| U.S. stock exposure | No eligible, approved route is available in Steward's public product. | Identify an actual permitted asset and venue, complete eligibility and legal review of the user-directed caregiver flow, and verify the route end to end. |

Steward's core does not rely on a broker partnership or business KYB for the Steward operator. That product choice does not remove user verification that an issuer, venue or applicable market may require. It also does not permit bypassing geographic, wallet or transfer restrictions. If a route needs business onboarding by Steward, it is outside the current accepted launch path.

The parent signs or authorizes actions through their own account; Steward's API prepares and checks requests but does not hold a financial signing key for the family. A caregiver's authority is bounded by the parent's rules. Each live asset route must preserve those controls and fail closed when required route or eligibility facts are unavailable.
